# Phase 40.13.6 — Exact online OWL Direct-Semantics vertical slice

Phase 40.13.6 introduces the online control and worker boundaries required to
execute exact OWL 2 Direct-Semantics checks with the pinned HermiT 1.4.5.519
adapter. It does not perform ontology alignment. Its inputs are already
ontology-grounded RDF graphs selected from an authorized dataset snapshot.

Only named graphs matching
`https://c8-next-generation.io/<scope>/<subdomain>/semkg` can enter the synthetic
asserted ontology. Corresponding `closure` graphs are derived acceleration
caches and `provenance` graphs are evidence; neither is silently promoted to
asserted OWL axioms. Imports must resolve to operator-pinned immutable
documents. Dataset, snapshot, authorized graph set, active RDF dataset,
datatype policy, OWL signature, profile, consistency, import, and synthetic
ontology hashes bind the request.

Legal BGPs route to a semantic index or finite closure only when independent
coverage evidence certifies that lane complete. Incomplete or unknown coverage
routes to exact HermiT. Illegal Direct-Semantics BGPs are rejected. Exact
candidate bindings are divided into deterministic partitions independent of
the current replica count, executed by bounded reasoner workers, and admitted
to the final proof/certificate merger only after every planned partition is
present and identity-verified.

The dedicated Kubernetes pool uses cgroup/cpuset-aware Rust, JVM, OpenMP, and
BLAS budgets. Its HPA considers CPU, memory, queued candidate partitions,
estimated axioms, queue age, and service latency. The coordinator/catalog pool
is unaffected and remains available. The default reasoner minimum is two;
scale-to-zero is rejected until durable queues and cold-start SLOs are
qualified.

This source increment is not the Phase 40.13.7 conformance milestone. The
standard `/sparql` response path still needs final binding to exact partition
dispatch and all 70 entailment-regime cases must be executed. Live multinode
HPA, node-autoscaler, retry, partial-worker, and chaos behavior also remains a
cluster qualification gate.
