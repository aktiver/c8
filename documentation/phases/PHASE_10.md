# Phase 10 — Context assembly and selective hydration

Phase 10 turns certified GUID bindings into an explainable graph slice. Required query/proof structure is never truncated. Optional ontology-directed neighbors and payload fields have explicit policy budgets and deterministic truncation metadata.

Hydration accepts only semantically qualified keys, resolves them through the snapshot-bound locator, rejects missing keys or mismatched snapshots, groups reads by exact object/row-group/column mask, and preserves query ordinals and multiplicity. Removing hydration fields can change representation only; it cannot change who qualifies for the answer. TriG/N-Quads export remains a terminal snapshot consumer, never an index dependency.
